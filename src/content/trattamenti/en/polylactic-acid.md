Poly-L-lactic acid is an injectable biostimulator, biocompatible and resorbable, which acts by stimulating fibroblasts to produce new collagen. Unlike traditional fillers, it does not produce immediate filling: it gradually improves the quality and firmness of the tissues, with a progressive result that becomes apparent over a few weeks.

The treatment can be performed on the face, décolleté, arms, elbows, knees and buttocks, to counteract the loss of tone and firmness and improve skin texture. The number of sessions and the amount of product are established after a careful clinical assessment, according to the area to be treated and the characteristics of the patient. The effects can last for more than a year and are influenced by metabolism and lifestyle. The aim is a natural, elegant and harmonious result, the outcome of a gradual improvement that is never artificial.
