Nose filler, or non-surgical rhinoplasty, is a minimally invasive aesthetic medicine procedure that makes it possible to correct some imperfections of the nose through small, targeted injections of hyaluronic acid.

The treatment is personalised on the basis of a careful analysis of the profile and proportions of the face, taking the patient’s preferences into account. It makes it possible to camouflage a small nasal hump, fill depressions of the dorsum and improve the projection and rotation of the tip. The aim is a more harmonious profile, with a natural and elegant result, in balance with the other features of the face.

The product is gradually resorbed: the effects last on average 12 months, depending on the type of filler, individual metabolism and lifestyle, in particular sun exposure, sporting activity and smoking.
