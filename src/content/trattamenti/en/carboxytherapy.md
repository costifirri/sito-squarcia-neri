Carboxytherapy is an aesthetic medicine treatment consisting of the controlled subcutaneous injection of medical carbon dioxide using microneedles. CO₂, a substance naturally produced by the body, causes local vasodilation and promotes the release of oxygen from haemoglobin to the tissues. This results in improved microcirculation and lymphatic drainage, and stimulation of cell metabolism and of collagen and elastin production, with firmer and more radiant skin.

The treatment is indicated for cellulite, localised fat deposits, skin laxity, dark circles under the eyes, stretch marks and alopecia, on both the face and the body. The protocol is personalised according to the patient’s objective, and the length of the sessions varies with the area treated: on average 30-40 minutes for cellulite of the limbs, less for biostimulation of the face, neck, décolleté or hands.
