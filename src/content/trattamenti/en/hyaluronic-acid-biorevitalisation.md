Biorevitalisation is an injectable treatment involving the intradermal administration of non-cross-linked hyaluronic acid. Unlike traditional fillers, it has no volumising function but acts by improving the quality of the skin. Thanks to its ability to bind water, hyaluronic acid helps to restore skin hydration and to stimulate the activity of fibroblasts, promoting the synthesis of collagen and elastin, with a progressive improvement in tone, firmness and radiance. The effects of the treatment are cumulative and improve over a course of sessions, the number and frequency of which are established according to the characteristics and needs of each patient.

It can be performed on the face, neck, décolleté and hands, as a single treatment or in combination with botulinum toxin, fillers and fractional laser, for fresher, more radiant skin with a natural appearance.
