Baby Glow is an injection technique that uses a hyaluronic acid filler to improve the quality of the skin and give it radiance, through eight targeted injection points of non-cross-linked hyaluronic acid, combined with amino acids, antioxidants, minerals and vitamins. Without changing the features of the face, it gives the skin a fresh, hydrated and naturally radiant appearance.
