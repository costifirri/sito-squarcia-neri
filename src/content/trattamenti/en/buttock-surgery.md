Gluteoplasty is the set of surgical procedures that make it possible to shape the buttocks, improving their volume, projection and shape, in harmony with the rest of the figure. The techniques available are lipofilling, the insertion of implants and the buttock lift, chosen according to the extent of the volume deficit and the condition of the tissues.

Lipofilling is indicated when the loss of volume is limited: the patient’s own fatty tissue, removed by liposuction and suitably processed, is regrafted in the emptier areas. When a greater increase in volume is required, specific implants are used, inserted through an incision in the upper part of the intergluteal cleft and placed beneath the gluteus maximus muscle. This position offers good coverage of the implant and a natural result; because the submuscular space is limited, the implants used are modest in size. In the presence of marked tissue laxity with buttock ptosis, a lift is performed instead, with removal of the excess skin.

The procedure is personalised after a thorough consultation, according to the anatomy, the quality of the tissues and the patient’s objectives, and can be combined, where indicated, with other procedures such as liposuction in the same session. Depending on the technique, it can be performed as a day-hospital procedure.

After surgery, a compression garment is worn for about three weeks. Normal daily activities can generally be resumed after 7-10 days, while it is advisable to refrain from sport for at least a month.
