Blepharoplasty is the surgical procedure that corrects excess skin and fat bags of the upper and lower eyelids, which are responsible for a tired, heavy look. These changes can appear with the passing of the years or have a constitutional component. Depending on the patient’s characteristics, the procedure may involve only the upper eyelids, only the lower ones or both, and is planned during a thorough consultation.

In upper blepharoplasty, the incision follows the natural crease of the eyelid, making the scar difficult to see. In lower blepharoplasty, when the excess mainly concerns the fat bags, a transconjunctival approach can be used, from inside the eyelid, with no skin scars; where there is excess skin, a fine incision just below the lashes is used instead. The eyelid generally heals quickly and with excellent outcomes.

The procedure is performed under local anaesthesia, possibly combined with sedation, and can be combined with other procedures, such as a brow lift or botulinum toxin in the periocular region, for a more complete rejuvenation of the eye area. The aim is a more open, fresh and rested look that preserves the expressiveness and identity of the face.
