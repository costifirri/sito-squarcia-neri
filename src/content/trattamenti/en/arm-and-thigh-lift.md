Over the years, reduced skin elasticity, changes in weight and less physical activity can leave the tissues of the arms and thighs loose and sagging. A limb lift makes it possible to remove the excess skin surgically and, where present, to reduce the accumulation of fat by liposuction, giving a more toned and harmonious outline.

It is a bespoke procedure, tailored to each patient: after a thorough consultation, the extent of the resection is planned according to the degree of skin laxity and the distribution of fatty tissue. In the arm, the scar is linear and runs along the inner surface, from the armpit towards the elbow. In the thigh, the resection may be limited to the groin crease or extend vertically along the inner surface. The incisions are placed in areas that are not very exposed; initially red, the scars tend to fade and soften over time.

At the end of the procedure a compression bandage is applied. Normal daily activities can generally be resumed after 7-10 days, while a longer period is needed for sport. In the first few weeks it is important to avoid excessive traction on the operated areas, to promote good scar quality. Swelling, small haematomas and seromas are common and, in most cases, resolve with appropriate management.
