Rhinoplasty is among the most requested procedures in plastic surgery. Because of its central position, the nose has a decisive influence on the appearance and balance of the face. The procedure reshapes the bony skeleton, the cartilages and, when necessary, the nasal septum: it makes it possible to correct the dorsum, reduce or reshape the tip, work on the alae and on asymmetries and treat deviation of the septum, with the aim of a harmonious aesthetic result and correct respiratory function.

Each procedure is designed individually, starting from a careful analysis of the face and a direct discussion with the patient about their expectations. Depending on the anatomy and the objectives, either the closed approach is chosen, with incisions inside the nostrils, or the open approach, with a small additional incision on the columella. The procedure is performed under general anaesthesia, as day surgery or with one night in hospital, and lasts on average 2-3 hours.

The final result can be appreciated after 6-12 months, once the swelling has subsided: a natural and harmonious nose, integrated with the features and respectful of the patient’s appearance.
