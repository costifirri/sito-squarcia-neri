With advancing age, the nasolabial folds, at the sides of the nose and mouth, and the marionette lines, which run downwards from the corners of the mouth, become progressively more evident. The injection of hyaluronic acid filler makes it possible to soften them, giving the face a fresher and firmer appearance.

The procedure is performed after a careful clinical assessment and planned according to the anatomy and characteristics of the patient’s face. It can be combined with filler in the cheekbone area: restoring the support of the midface, in synergy with the treatment of the folds, contributes to a more balanced correction. The aim is always a result that preserves the expressiveness and identity of the face.

The product is gradually resorbed: the effects last on average 6 to 12 months, depending on the type of filler, individual metabolism and lifestyle, in particular sun exposure, sporting activity and smoking.
