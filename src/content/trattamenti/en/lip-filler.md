Lip filler is an aesthetic medicine procedure involving the injection of hyaluronic acid, a molecule naturally present in the body, with marked hydrating and volumising properties.

The treatment is personalised on the basis of a careful analysis of the lip profile, the shape of the face and the patient’s preferences. It makes it possible to redefine the contour, increase volume in a measured way and, where indicated, soften the vertical perioral lines, the so-called “barcode” lines, and those at the corners of the mouth. The aim is a result that enhances individual features while respecting the balance of the face.

The product is gradually resorbed: the effects last on average 6 to 12 months, depending on the type of filler, individual metabolism and lifestyle, in particular sun exposure, sporting activity and smoking.
