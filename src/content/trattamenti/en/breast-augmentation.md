Breast augmentation is the surgical procedure that increases the volume of the breast and improves its shape through the insertion of breast implants. It is indicated in cases of underdeveloped breasts, breast asymmetry in volume or shape, or loss of volume after pregnancy, breastfeeding or significant weight loss. It is advisable to have it performed after reaching adulthood, when development of the mammary gland is complete and the decision can be made with full awareness.

The implant can be placed in front of the pectoralis major muscle, in the subglandular position, or using the dual plane technique, that is, behind the muscle in the upper part and behind the gland in the lower part. The subglandular position is suitable for breasts with well-represented tissues. The dual plane is preferable in the presence of thin tissues and a poorly developed gland, because it offers better coverage of the implant and a more natural result.

There is no implant or technique that is right for everyone: the procedure is personalised for each patient. During the consultation, the anatomy, the quality of the tissues and the patient’s wishes are assessed, in order to choose together the most suitable type and volume of implant, position and technique. Latest-generation implants offer high standards of quality and durability, but regular clinical check-ups remain essential after surgery. The aim is a harmonious and natural breast, in balance with the figure.
