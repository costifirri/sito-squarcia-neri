Over time, the loss of volume and support in the tissues of the face can leave the cheekbones looking hollow or poorly defined; in other cases, limited projection is a constitutional feature. The injection of hyaluronic acid makes it possible to increase the projection of the cheekbone area and restore the volumes of the midface, with a supporting and lifting effect on the tissues.

The result is personalised and defined together with the patient on the basis of a careful analysis of the anatomy and proportions of the face. The aim is always a natural result, never artificial, in harmony with individual features.

The product is gradually resorbed: the effects last on average 6 to 12 months, depending on the type of filler, individual metabolism and lifestyle, in particular sun exposure, sporting activity and smoking.
