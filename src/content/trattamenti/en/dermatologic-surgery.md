Dermatologic surgery, or skin surgery, is one of the branches of reconstructive plastic surgery and deals with the removal of lesions of the skin and subcutaneous tissue, such as moles, cysts, lipomas, keratoses and skin tumours. The plastic surgeon combines complete removal with attention to the aesthetic result: the choice of the site and orientation of the incision, respect for the skin tension lines and, when necessary, the use of local flaps make it possible to obtain scars that are as discreet as possible, especially in exposed areas such as the face.

Each procedure is preceded by a careful assessment of the patient’s history and clinical examination of the lesion, which makes it possible to define the indication and plan the most appropriate technique. The procedures are performed on an outpatient basis, under local anaesthesia, and the tissue removed is always sent for histopathological examination, which is essential for the definitive diagnosis and for planning any subsequent check-ups.
